What we do

Three services, each one a complete answer to a specific problem: employing people legally, adding engineers to your team, or building the software itself.

Employer of Record (EOR)

End-to-end legal employment, payroll, tax compliance and HR administration for foreign companies hiring Armenian talent without establishing a local entity. Contracts, onboarding and statutory benefits are administered under the Labour Code of Armenia.

€199 / month per employee

Outstaffing & staff augmentation

Dedicated software engineers, developers, QA specialists and IT talent embedded directly into your team. You keep technical management; we run the operational side — hardware, workspace and local retention.

Developer salary + €199 / month

Custom software engineering

Full-cycle web and mobile application engineering, enterprise backend design, cloud architecture and legacy system modernisation, delivered by our own team in Yerevan.

$35 – $65 / hour by seniority

How it works

Four steps from first call to someone working in your team.

Scope the role

A 30-minute call to agree the role, seniority, budget and start date. We come back with a written proposal and the realistic hiring timeline for that profile in Armenia.

Sign the agreement

One service agreement covering scope, rates, IP ownership and data protection. No set-up fee and no minimum headcount.

Hire and onboard

We issue the Armenian labour contract, register the employee, arrange equipment and workspace, and run the first payroll cycle.

Run and support

Monthly payroll, tax and pension filings, HR administration and retention support. You manage the work; we keep the employment compliant.
